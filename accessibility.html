Accessibility statement

gal.tidhar.org.il · Last updated: 4 September 2026

Commitment

This site aims to be usable by everyone, including people who rely on a keyboard, a screen reader, or magnification. It targets conformance with WCAG 2.1/2.2 level AA.

What's in place

  • Semantic HTML with a single <main> landmark, headings in order, and a "skip to content" link.
  • Keyboard-operable navigation with visible focus indicators.
  • Text contrast checked against AA, and layout that reflows without horizontal scrolling down to small screens.
  • Images carry alternative text; the site works with JavaScript limited.

Known limitations

Some blog posts contain code samples and technical diagrams whose fine detail may not fully translate to a screen reader; the surrounding text explains them. If you hit a barrier anywhere on the site, it is treated as a bug, not an edge case.

Report a barrier

Found something that blocks you? Email gal@tidhar.org.il with the page and what happened, and it gets fixed. In the EU you may also raise accessibility concerns with the relevant national authority.
