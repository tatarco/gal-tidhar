WHAT I STOPPED MEASURING
How many cars are waiting
A ferry here takes 100+ vehicles and the camera sees a small part of them. Any number I print is falsely precise. Gal's rule: never print a count.
WHAT I MEASURE INSTEAD
Is this patch of road
still road
Appearance, not detection. The fraction of pixels that no longer match an empty-road reference for that hour. Two humps, and the threshold sits in the valley.
