three anchors, all computed before I say a word
what actually sets the number
floor
what it costs me to deliver. Never the price - it only tells me where "I would rather not" begins.
value
what this phase is worth to the client in year one.
alternative
what they would pay otherwise. An agency, a hire, or doing nothing.
Value and alternative set the price. The floor only holds a veto.
